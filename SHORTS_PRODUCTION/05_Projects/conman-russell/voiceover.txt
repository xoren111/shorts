This man walked out of a maximum-security prison four times. The guards held the door.
First: dressed as a workman, he tapped a police radio on the guards' window — and walked out.
Second: posing as a judge, he phoned the jail and cut his own bail from nine hundred thousand to forty-five thousand.
Third: he colored his uniform green with markers. The guard saw a doctor — and opened the front door.
Fourth: fake medical records convinced the prison he was dying. They sent him to a hospice. Then Texas got the news: Steven Russell was dead.
He wasn't.
Posing as a doctor, he'd checked himself out — and declared himself dead.
The one mistake that caught him? He couldn't stay away from the man he loved.
Texas gave him one hundred forty-four years. One hour a day outside his cell. Because…
