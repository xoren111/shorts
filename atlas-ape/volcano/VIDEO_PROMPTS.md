# Humans vs Volcano — промпты для генерации видео (B-roll)

Каждый кадр генерируется отдельно: 9:16, 5–8 с, 2–3 варианта на кадр. Блок STYLE вставлять в КАЖДЫЙ промпт без
изменений — так все клипы будут в одном стиле. Карты, цифры, субтитры, табло и Atlas делаем сами в монтаже,
поэтому в кадрах нет текста. Стиль — яркая стилизованная реконструкция, а не «архивная хроника»: это честно
(событий никто так не снимал) и подходит к мультяшному ведущему. Реальных людей (Паттон) не показываем в лицо.
Нижняя треть кадра темнее и проще — туда встанут субтитры и Atlas.

Привязка к озвучке Bradley (~67 с): хук 0–4 · Гавайи 4–20 · Италия 20–33 · Исландия 33–58 · финал 58–67.

## STYLE (вставлять в каждый промпт)
Vibrant stylized cinematic animation, not photorealistic: rich saturated colors, glowing molten lava in orange,
gold and hot magenta against deep teal, indigo and violet skies, dramatic rim light, volumetric smoke, embers and
steam, painterly textures with crisp detail and bold shapes, epic but playful tone. Vertical 9:16 frame, main action
in the upper two thirds, bottom third darker and uncluttered. Smooth dynamic camera. No text, no letters, no
numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no recognizable faces of real people.

## Кадры
1. HOOK 0–4 с — A 1930s propeller biplane bomber flies low over an erupting volcano at sunset; small bombs tumble
   from its belly and burst into a glowing river of lava below in fountains of sparks. Fast low-angle tracking shot
   following the plane, then the camera tilts down into the lava explosion.
2. HAWAII 4–10 с — Night. A wide river of glowing lava snakes down the dark slopes of a huge shield volcano toward
   the lights of a small 1930s bay town with palm trees and a calm moonlit bay. Slow aerial push-in from behind the
   lava front toward the town.
3. HAWAII 10–16 с — Daylight 1935. A formation of 1930s US Army biplane bombers in olive drab with round markings
   flies over black lava fields; bombs drop and hit the lava channel, throwing up bursts of molten rock and smoke.
   Side tracking shot alongside the planes.
4. HAWAII 16–20 с — The lava front slowly stops and darkens into black crust, wisps of smoke rising, while a 1930s
   scientist in a hat, seen from behind as a silhouette, watches through binoculars from a rocky ridge. Calm, slightly
   ironic mood. Slow pull-back.
5. ITALY 20–25 с — Dusk on a giant Sicilian volcano: a long glowing lava flow pours down the slope toward a small
   stone town with terracotta roofs, vineyards and olive trees. Sweeping aerial drone shot.
6. ITALY 25–29 с — 1990s yellow bulldozers have built a tall earth wall across the valley; a wave of glowing lava
   rises behind it and spills right over the top in a slow molten waterfall. Low ground-level shot looking up at the
   wall.
7. ITALY 29–33 с — High on the volcano near the vent, a huge controlled explosion bursts open a lava tube with a
   flash and a cloud of rock dust; the glowing flow turns and pours into a new channel away from the town. Wide shot,
   slight camera shake on the blast.
8. ICELAND 33–41 с — Winter night 1973 on a small North Atlantic island: a long fissure tears open behind a fishing
   town of colorful houses covered in snow, a curtain of fire fountains lights the sky; fishing boats with lights
   leave the harbor in a line across black water. Wide shot from the sea.
9. ICELAND 41–50 с — Rescue workers in 1970s rain gear aim massive hoses and a ship's water cannons at a towering
   wall of black and glowing lava next to a harbor; seawater hits the lava and explodes into giant white clouds of
   steam lit orange from inside. Dramatic orbiting camera.
10. ICELAND 50–58 с — Dawn: a huge dark ridge of cooled lava, still steaming, stands right beside the harbor
    entrance, which stays open; fishing boats glide back into the calm harbor, colorful houses, pink and gold sky.
    Slow triumphant aerial rise.
11. FINAL 58–67 с (по желанию, фон для табло) — Three volcanoes side by side under a starry violet sky, gently
    glowing, calm after the battle, embers drifting up. Slow push-in; leave the center open for a scoreboard.

## Если генератор поддерживает negative prompt
text, letters, subtitles, captions, watermark, logo, UI, blurry, low resolution, distorted planes, extra wings,
deformed hands, real celebrity faces, gore, modern cars in historical scenes.
