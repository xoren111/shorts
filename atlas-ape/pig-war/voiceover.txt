America and Britain almost went to war. Over one pig.
A farmer caught it eating his potatoes. So he shot it.
Problem: the farmer was American. The pig was British.
He offered ten dollars. The owner wanted a hundred.
Worse, both countries claimed the island.
America sent over four hundred soldiers. Britain sent five warships.
The governor wanted the Americans gone. The admiral said no. Not over a pig.
So both sides just stayed. For twelve years. And partied together.
Then a German emperor settled it. The island went to America.
Total casualties: one pig.
Team farmer, or team pig?
