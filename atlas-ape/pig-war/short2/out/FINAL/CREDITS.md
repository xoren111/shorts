## Terrain (map / mapfinal shots)
Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen/Tilezen) — data from SRTM, USGS 3DEP/NED, GMTED2010, ETOPO1, Canadian CDEM; see tilezen/joerd attribution
(https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md). Terrarium PNG tiles, zoom 12 (land) + zoom 8 (water depth), from
https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/terrarium/{z}/{x}/{y}.png (Registry of Open Data on AWS) — fetched by tools/geo_pnw.py
into data/terrain/pnw/.
