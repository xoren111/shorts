## 2. Description (copy in full)

```
America and Britain almost went to war. Over one pig. 🐷

June 1859, San Juan Island: American farmer Lyman Cutlar caught a pig eating his potatoes and shot it. The pig belonged to Charles Griffin, who ran the British Hudson's Bay Company sheep farm. Cutlar offered $10. Griffin wanted $100.

The island was claimed by both countries, so the quarrel escalated: 461 American soldiers with 14 cannons faced five Royal Navy warships with 70 guns and 2,140 men. Governor James Douglas wanted the Americans out — but Rear Admiral Robert Baynes refused to start a war "over a squabble about a pig".

Both sides stayed on the island for 12 years and visited each other's camps for national holidays and sports. In 1872 German Emperor Wilhelm I's arbitration gave the islands to the United States.

Total casualties: one pig.
Bonus: American park rangers still raise the British flag at English Camp on San Juan Island every day.

Team farmer or team pig? 👇
```
