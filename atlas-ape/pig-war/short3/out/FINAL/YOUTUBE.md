The Pig War, 1859: America and Britain almost went to war over ONE pig 🐷

June 1859, San Juan Island: American farmer Lyman Cutlar caught a pig eating his potatoes — and shot it. The pig belonged to Charles Griffin of the British Hudson's Bay Company. Cutlar offered $10. Griffin wanted $100.

Both countries claimed the island, so it escalated fast: 461 American soldiers with 14 cannons against five Royal Navy warships with 70 guns and 2,140 men. Governor James Douglas wanted the Americans gone — but Rear Admiral Robert Baynes refused to start a war "over a squabble about a pig".

So both sides just stayed. For 12 years. They even partied together on national holidays. In 1872, German Emperor Wilhelm I's arbitration gave the islands to the United States.

Total casualties: one pig.
