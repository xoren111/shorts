# Owner's rules for Atlas Ape Shorts (from 8 review rounds, 18–21.10.2026) — read before touching any shot

Picture
- Every shot must be instantly UNDERSTANDABLE with the sound off: show the thing itself, close, with one clear action
  readable in ~1.5 s (stomp → reveal, kick → keeper → net). Far/tiny staging, abstract diagrams, unexplained changes = rejected.
- Look = lit stylised 3D (EEVEE ray tracing, sunset HDRI light, violet→peach sky gradient for the camera, Poly Haven CC0
  textures, Kenney Mini Characters). Nothing flat-cheap, no stick figures.
- Smoke/dust/clouds = real volumes (yf2.volume_box / plume / cloud2), never piles of spheres. Thin trails/jets = mesh tubes
  with a fake-volume shader.
- Nothing may blink or pop unexplained; motion smooth; cuts are 0.4 s cross-dissolves (no flashes, light leaks, whips, shake).
- No real archive photos dropped into 3D shots.
- Bright colours must not vanish on similar backgrounds (black pig on dark soil → lighter soil / rim light).

Text
- Subtitles: centred, 54 px, ~0.92 opacity, ONLY while speaking, never in the bottom 15 % (YouTube overlay).
- Never show the same thing twice: no headline/sticker that echoes the voice. At most ONE calm horizontal label besides the
  subtitle, only if it adds info the voice does not say (a name, a date, a number).
- A key number may be shown BIG (KeyFact) — then the subtitle of that phrase is hidden.
- Big text always has an ink outline + dark scrim (readable on bright skies). No rolling counters.

Atlas (orangutan host): only in the ending here (reaction, never decoration), above the bottom 20 %.
Sound: SFX for every visual event; channel music bed «Frame-Dragging» −21.6 dB + sidechain under the voice (tools/mix.py).
