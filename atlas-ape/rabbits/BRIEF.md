# Brief — «Australia vs Rabbits» (The Atlas Ape, episode 2)

## Channel & format
- The Atlas Ape: English vertical Shorts (1080×1920) about wild real geography/history; host = Atlas, a cartoon orangutan in a
  fedora. Episode 1 «Humans vs Volcano» (~/shorts/atlas-ape/volcano/SCRIPT.md + voiceover.txt — read both for voice and rhythm):
  three human attempts against lava, punchy lines, ended «Final score: Humans two. Volcano zero. And Hawaii? We don't talk
  about Hawaii.»
- This episode continues the «Humans vs …» series in spirit: humans try weapon after weapon against 24 rabbits' descendants.
- Narration voice = ElevenLabs «Silas – Weathered Villain» (model v4): deep, slow, dry, sardonic. Measured pace on episode 1:
  **2.0 words per second including pauses, 11.5 characters per second**. Every sentence break adds a pause.
- **Length budget: 52–60 s → 105–120 words, ≤ 690 characters of narration** (ep. 1 was 151 words = 75.8 s, too long).
- Audience: all ages. No gore, no sick/dead animals, no hunting imagery, no poison talk with images of suffering. Viruses =
  abstract diagrams/maps. The 2002 film «Rabbit-Proof Fence» (Stolen Generations) stays out.
- Music: one calm channel bed, very quiet. Drama comes from SFX and editing, not music.

## Owner's rules (from 4 rounds of notes on ep. 1 — mandatory)
1. Hook in the first second, no intro. One visual event per sentence; nothing static > 2–2.5 s; smooth cross-dissolves (no flashes,
   whips, shakes, light leaks).
2. Never show the same thing twice: no headline/sticker that echoes the subtitle or voice. Besides the small subtitle, at most ONE
   calm horizontal label on screen, and only if it ADDS info the voice does not say at that moment (a place name, a date, a unit
   conversion). No rolling counters, no chips, no stamps.
3. Key numbers MAY appear big (e.g. «1,830 km») — then the subtitle of that phrase is hidden. Mark these as KEYFACT.
4. Atlas (on-screen host, lip-synced to the narration) appears ONLY in three windows: the hook, ONE key story moment, and the ending.
   Available acting: emotions neutral/happy/excited/surprised/skeptical/suspicious/sarcastic/confused/thinking/laughing/sad/angry/
   sleepy; gestures point, point_stick (pointer), shrug, facepalm, thumbs_up, wink, nod, laugh, surprise_reaction, show_globe,
   count_three, ok_sign, peace_sign, phone_look, thinking, look_left/right/up, wave; props globe/map/pointer/phone/magnifier.
   Head views front / three-quarter / profile. Nothing else may be invented for him.
5. Look: bright cartoon «toon» 3D (Blender) with a VIOLET dusk sky, cream clouds, bright greens/ochres — approved by the owner —
   plus animated flat cutaway diagrams for mechanisms, real archive photos (Ken Burns), and maps/globe dives. No AI video, no AI
   images.
6. Facts: only from ~/shorts/atlas-ape/rabbits/FACTS.md, in its SAFE wording (respect hedges like «estimated», «probably»,
   «scientists say», «most»). Its «DO NOT SAY» list is binding.
