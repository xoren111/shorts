Thirteen rabbits got on a ship, family records say.
Twenty-four got off.
Turns out, rabbits can multiply.
Twenty-four became hundreds of millions.
Australia fought back.
Round one: a prize.
Louis Pasteur entered. With chicken cholera.
The rules protected sheep, horses, dogs.
They forgot birds.
It didn't catch on. Nobody won.
Round two: a fence. Ocean to ocean.
Before it was finished, rabbits were on the safe side.
Some scratched to get back out.
Round three: a rabbit virus, locked on an island.
It escaped, most likely on flies.
Next year: an official release ceremony.
The virus was already there.
And it worked.
Three rounds: Rabbits two. Flies one. Australia zero.
They were winning before the ship even arrived. Probably.
