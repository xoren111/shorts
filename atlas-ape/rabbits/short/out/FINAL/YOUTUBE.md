## Description (paste as is)
Christmas Day 1859: a ship brought 24 rabbits to Australia — family records say only 13 got on. By 1950 there were an estimated
600 million. Australia fought back three times: a £25,000 prize (Louis Pasteur entered with chicken cholera), a rabbit-proof
fence from ocean to ocean that the rabbits beat before it was finished, and a rabbit virus that escaped its island quarantine,
most likely on flies. Final score: Rabbits 2, Flies 1, Australia 0. Today there are still an estimated 200 million.

Sources: Alves et al. 2022, PNAS 119(35) · CSIRO · National Museum of Australia · State Library of Western Australia ·
J.S. Crawford, WA Dept of Agriculture (1969) · Australian Academy of Science · University of Melbourne · CSIROpedia ·
Schwensow et al. 2014 · Government of Western Australia media statement, 18 Oct 1996 · Invasive Species Council.
