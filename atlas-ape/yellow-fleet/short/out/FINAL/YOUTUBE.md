## Description (no links — new channel)
In June 1967, a convoy of cargo ships from 8 countries was inside the Suez Canal when the Six-Day War broke out. Egypt sank ships to block the canal, and 14 of them were trapped in the Great Bitter Lake — right on the front line — for almost eight years.

Sandstorms dusted them yellow: the Yellow Fleet. Crews from both sides of the Cold War took turns aboard, became friends, played football on deck, made their own stamps and held their own Olympics (Poland won).

In May 1975 only two, the West German Münsterland and Nordwind, could sail home on their own. Tens of thousands welcomed them in Hamburg. Part of the cargo: kitchen clocks — delivered only eight years late.
