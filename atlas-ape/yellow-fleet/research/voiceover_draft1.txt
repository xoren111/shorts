This ship blocked the Suez Canal for six days.
These ships were stuck there for eight years.
Mid-canal, a war broke out. It lasted six days.
But Egypt sank ships to block the canal.
Fourteen ships, East and West, trapped on the front line.
So Cold War enemies became neighbors. Then friends.
Football on deck. Movie nights.
Their own Olympics. Poland won.
Even their own stamps.
Sandstorms dusted them yellow. The Yellow Fleet.
The sailors took turns.
Only two ships could still sail home on their own.
Hamburg. Tens of thousands cheered.
Only eight years late.
Would you have taken a turn?
