high-res portrait).

## 2. Great Bitter Lake Association stamps / covers

- **None of these exist freely.** Commons has no GBLA stamp or cover. Searches for GBLA, «Bitter Lake» stamp/cover/label,
  Briefmarke and Bittersee all came up empty.
- ru.wikipedia shows one label (`Gbla13.jpg`, «500 days», 1968) as a **local fair-use file** (not on Commons), sourced from
  shipsonstamps.org: https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Файл:Gbla13.jpg. Not reusable.
- Other images are all rights-reserved: shipsonstamps.org (© Bjoern Moritz) http://www.shipsonstamps.org/Topics/html/gbla.htm
  · National Museums Liverpool, envelopes from *Agapenor* 1968, DX/2532
  https://www.liverpoolmuseums.org.uk/artifact/airmail-envelopes-sent-agapenor-blue-funnel-ocean-steamship-company
  («© National Museums Liverpool. All rights reserved») · David Feldman auction lot 35437 · Corinphila lot 571.
- **Designers:** no individual names are published. The labels were made by the crews of the 14 ships in their spare
  time. Some commercial labels, incl. a 1968 «G.B.L. Olympics» label, were printed ashore by ship chandlers. One Nefertiti
  overprint is evidently Polish, and one design is credited to a French seaman of *Sindh*. Each ship had a uniform
  **«Mailed on Board» rubber cachet**: an anchor with «14», double stripes for the canal, «GBLA» and the ship's name. From
  1969 there were group cachets and a buoy cachet. A cancellation office met on Sundays aboard *Nordwind*. Reference
  catalogue: Capt. Bryan Hill, *Postage Stamps of the Great Bitter Lake Association* (Picton, 1975). FACTS.md D1 has the
  linocut and sewing-machine details.
- **Copyright:** these are anonymous crew works from 1967–75, probably still protected in the EU/UK (70 years). Treat
  them as **not free**.
- → **Recommendation for line 9:** use the planned flat «how the stamp was made» schematic and design an *original* stamp
  from the generic elements (anchor + «14», two canal stripes, «GBLA», ship name, yellow ink, sewing-machine
  perforation). Do not trace a real label. **Drop «real GBLA stamp (Ken Burns)» from SCRIPT line 9.**
- Related stamp: *Egyptian Stamp for Suez Canal Opening in 1975.jpg* (Commons, «own work» CC BY-SA 4.0) shows an
  **Alamy watermark**, so it is probably copyvio. **Avoid.**

## Fact flags for the script team

- «Essberger Chemist» is **not** in the 15 (EN/DE Wikipedia, FACTS.md B2). Don't label any toon ship with it.
- *Sindh* was later renamed *Essayons* under Norwegian ownership. Wikipedia lists her as «Essayons (ex-Sindh)».
- The Commons photos «Suezship.JPG» and Dan Hadani 1969 show the **Observer in Lake Timsah**. Caption them as «a
  stranded ship», never as «the 14 in the Great Bitter Lake».
- Fricke (1) is dated **24 May 1975**, which matches FACTS E2.
