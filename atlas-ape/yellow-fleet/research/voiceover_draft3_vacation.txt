Fourteen ships entered the Suez Canal for a one-day trip.
They got out eight years later.
Halfway through, war broke out.
Egypt sank ships to block the canal.
And the canal became the front line.
Trapped in a lake, nowhere to go.
Sandstorms dusted them yellow. The Yellow Fleet.
Their countries were rivals. The sailors became friends.
Football on deck. Their own stamps.
Their own Olympics. Poland won.
The ships stayed. The crews changed every few months.
In the end, just two could still sail.
Home to Hamburg. Tens of thousands cheered.
Only eight years late.
A few months there: vacation, or prison?
