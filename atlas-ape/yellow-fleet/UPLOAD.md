## 2. Описание (копировать целиком)

```
14 ships went into the Suez Canal for a one-day trip. They got out 8 years later.

June 1967: a convoy of cargo ships was inside the canal when the Six-Day War broke out. Egypt sank ships to block the canal, and 14 of them were stuck in the Great Bitter Lake, right on the front line, for almost eight years. Desert sandstorms dusted them yellow, so they became the Yellow Fleet.

The ships came from 8 countries on both sides of the Cold War. Their sailors, about 3,000 of them taking turns over the years, became friends: football on deck, their own stamps, even their own Olympics in 1968. Poland won.

In May 1975 only two ships, the West German Münsterland and Nordwind, could sail home on their own. Tens of thousands welcomed them in Hamburg. Part of the cargo: kitchen clocks. Delivered only eight years late.

The Ever Given blocked the Suez Canal for 6 days in 2021. These ships waited 8 years.

How many stars would you give this delivery? ⭐

Sources: Capt. Bryan Hill, "Postage Stamps of the Great Bitter Lake Association" (1975) · American Shipper, Aug 1975 (Hapag-Lloyd account, reprinted by FreightWaves) · Der Spiegel (2008, captains' accounts) · mare No. 40 (2003) · Wikipedia: Yellow Fleet.
```

## 6. Когда и что делать после публикации

- **Время:** 17:00–19:00 по Еревану (09:00–11:00 в Нью-Йорке), чтобы первые часы пришлись на утро в США.
- **Сразу после публикации** закрепить вопрос-угадайку (отвечать легко, ответы смешные):
  ```
  Guess what ELSE was in the cargo besides kitchen clocks 👇
  ```
  Через 2–3 часа ответить под ним разгадкой (FACTS E4):
  ```
  Almost 8 MILLION eggs 🥚 + 300 tons of pears. The tins burst over the years, but the wool and steel arrived just fine.
  ```
  Готовые ответы на частые вопросы:
  - «They were stuck for 8 years?!» → `The ships were. The crews took turns: about 3,000 sailors served a few months each.`
  - «Why didn't they just leave?» → `Egypt sank ships at both ends of the canal, and the lake sat right on the front line.`
  - «Were the stamps real?» → `Homemade! Most letters still needed Egyptian stamps, but a few made it home with only theirs.`
- В первый час ответить на каждый комментарий: YouTube учитывает раннюю активность.
- **Через 24 и 72 часа** посмотреть в Studio «Viewed vs swiped away» (цель ≥ 70 %) и средний % просмотра (цель ≥ 80 %),
  прислать мне цифры.
- Если за 48 часов меньше 1 000 просмотров, ролик **не перезаливать**: это результат теста, выходит следующий ролик.
