He sold everything and rode a bus 2,000+ km across Brazil to meet the farm widow he followed online. Nobody there had ever seen her… 💔

He says he'll go back to the same place. Should he? 👇

The story: in October 2026 a 49-year-old bricklayer from Imperatriz (Maranhão) travelled to Belo Horizonte to meet "Jéssica Fazendeira", a farm widow with 300,000+ followers. A local radio station told him the truth: she was an AI-generated character. Her anonymous creator says the account was made to sell products. Back home, he said he would try again.
Reported by Rádio Itatiaia, TV Mirante, g1, UOL and Dexerto.
