**Description:**
October 7, 1916: Georgia Tech beat Cumberland College 222–0, the most lopsided score in college football history.
Cumberland had dropped football, but cancelling meant a $3,000 penalty, so they sent law students and frat brothers.
Georgia Tech's coach was John Heisman. The Heisman Trophy is named after him.
Bonus: the game ball, with the score written on it, sold in 2014 for $40,388.
