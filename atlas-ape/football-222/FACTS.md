DO NOT SAY (myths or unproven, flagged by Wikipedia):
- «Cumberland never gained a single yard» — myth; their longest play was a 10-yard pass and they gained yards on at least six plays.
- «Tech scored on every play» — myth; it scored on every one of its sets of downs.
- «Heisman went for exactly 222 on purpose» — only speculation («It is speculated…»); at most a wink in the picture, never in the voice or a label.
- «Ten times their 22» — false arithmetic (10 × 22 = 220).
- «Cumberland used professionals» as a fact — only «allegations»; keep «allegedly».
- injuries: the quarterback was carried off with concussions and a player had a facial injury — do not show or dramatise.
- «15 turnovers» — Wikipedia says «purportedly»; skip.
- «a fine» from a court — it was a contract payment («penalty» in the voice is a fair simplification).

Bonus for the description: the game ball (score written on it) sold in 2014 for $40,388; at a 1956 reunion a Cumberland player
said one play saved them from losing 229–0.
